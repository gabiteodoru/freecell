TrickCam Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2025

Data Collection

TrickCam does not collect, store, or share any personal information or user data. All camera processing and pose analysis occurs locally on your device in real-time.

Camera Access

TrickCam requires camera permission to provide delayed video replay and pose analysis features. Camera data is processed in real-time on your device and is never saved, transmitted, or shared. No photos or videos are stored.

Data Storage

App preferences (delay settings, camera selection, etc.) are stored locally on your device. This information never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third parties.

Third-Party Services

This app does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or data collection services.
